Crystal Caverns!

Dash through glowing caves, jump across gaps, grab crystals and bring your haul home to build a hamster mining colony.

Move: A / D or ← / → · Jump: Space / ↑ · Dash: Shift · Pause: P

🏡 Your Burrow

Spend collected crystals to expand your colony. Each upgrade generates more simulated SQK after every completed run. The mine works only when you finish a run, not in the background.

📖 How the tokenomics work

Your colony starts at 1,000 fictional mining power. Wheel upgrades add +500 power; a drill adds +1,500; a reactor adds +4,000. The game's simplified reward for each completed run is floor(6,900 × your power ÷ 100,000) game SQK. The 6,900 figure is a proposed whitepaper reward parameter, not a live block payout. Network power is an invented constant; the upgrade values, crystal economy and completed-run payout are game mechanics, not Squeekchain protocol rules. This prototype does not connect to any wallet or blockchain, mint NFTs, or distribute real tokens.

The separate SqueekLab concept focuses on analysis. SqueekRun is its fictional arcade companion. Source for protocol concepts: Squeekchain whitepaper.
